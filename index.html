About

An educator who leads with data — and with people.

I'm a Case Manager at Carrie Jacobs Bond Elementary in Chicago Public Schools, where I lead our Behavioral Health Team, sit on the Instructional Leadership Team, and coordinate services for students across the full range of disabilities. Before Chicago, I taught in a Primary Emotional Disturbance Unit in Ohio and completed my training across five schools in Georgia.

What ties all of it together is the same conviction: students with disabilities deserve rigorous instruction, not lowered expectations — and the adults around them deserve real support to deliver it. As a case manager, that's meant coaching first-year cluster teachers, writing compliant and meaningful IEPs, and helping staff use data to make better decisions. As an administrator, I want to build that culture across the whole school.

I'm currently completing my administrative licensure with the goal of moving into an Assistant Principal or Principal role — ideally one where special education isn't a separate program, but part of how the whole school community thinks about instruction.
